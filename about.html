About

HBO's Getting On Television
Amazon's Girl's Night Television
Rogue Lantern Owner, Las Vegas

When Adam was five, his dad began teaching him multiplication in a closet crawl space because that is what an Indian dad does. His white mom wrote music while holding down a day job as a nurse.

He grew up in a small town outside of Philly called Phoenixville, which was gentrified after he left?! When he moved to Los Angeles, he landed a role playing Adam on HBO's Getting On. He also played the flamboyant Seabass on Amazon's Girl's Night, directed by a showrunner from The Big Bang Theory.

Adam has performed stand-up all over Los Angeles and Atlanta, including venues such as Flappers in Burbank and The Laughing Skull in Atlanta. He is also the owner of Rogue Lantern in Las Vegas.
